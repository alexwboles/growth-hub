Nine independent AI micro-products. Each stands alone — they just happen to answer the same question: how do I get more customers?

The products

How they connect

Act 1 — Get found

SEOCheck audits your local presence and tells you what to fix first; SocialSpark keeps you visible week after week — one job photo becomes seven ready-to-post updates. No point winning a customer if they never find you.

Act 2 — Win the customer

Once you're visible, LeadQualify catches the visitor on your website and scores them hot, warm, or cold; AdCopy fills the funnel with paid traffic that actually reads like you; and PricingPilot prices every quote to win the job and protect your margin. Traffic is only worth what you convert.

Act 3 — Keep them & multiply

A won customer is an asset: ReviewPilot turns them into public 5-star proof, LoyaltyLoop turns proof into repeat visits, and ReferralPilot turns repeat buyers into new customers. WinBack catches the ones that fell through the cracks — lapsed buyers, quiet leads, anyone who went dark — and pulls them back into the loop.

Honest note

These are independent products, not one platform. The three acts are a playbook, not a package — buy one, bundle a few, or take all nine where it genuinely makes sense for your business. No forced connections.
